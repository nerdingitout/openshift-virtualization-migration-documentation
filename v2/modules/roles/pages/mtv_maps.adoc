= mtv_maps

Creates MTV/Forklift StorageMap and NetworkMap custom resources on the destination OpenShift cluster for one source–target pair. Used from xref:journey:create-maps.adoc[Create maps].

Playbook: `infra.openshift_virtualization_migration.vmf_mtv_maps` (`playbooks/vmf_mtv_maps.yml` on the collection `v2` branch).

The role queries the MTV inventory (it does not need source hypervisor credentials), then applies the map CRs. Full argument list: link:https://github.com/redhat-cop/openshift_virtualization_migration/blob/v2/roles/mtv_maps/README.md[mtv_maps README].
